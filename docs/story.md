# The Mythos: "The Keeper of the Spark"

**The Core Truth:**
*Knowledge is living light. But the natural state of the universe is darkness (The Fade). Your job is not to "study"—it is to keep the lights on.*

---

## Part 1: The Origin (First App Launch)

*Use this script for your Onboarding Screens. It sets the emotional stakes immediately.*

### Screen 1: The Void

* **Visual:** The screen is pitch black (OLED dark). Silence.
* **Text (Fades in slowly):** *"The mind is a vast, dark ocean."*
* **Action:** The user must tap the screen to continue.

### Screen 2: The First Spark

* **Visual:** Where the user tapped, a tiny, fragile glowing dot appears. It flickers weakly, like a dying candle.
* **Text:** *"A single idea is fragile. Alone, it flickers... and eventually fades."*
* **Action:** A second dot appears nearby. The user is prompted to drag a line between them.

### Screen 3: The Connection

* **Action:** As the user connects them, a **humming snap** sounds. The line glows neon blue. Both dots suddenly stabilize and shine brighter.
* **Text:** *"But connected... they become brilliant."*

### Screen 4: The Call to Action

* **Visual:** The camera zooms out to reveal a massive, empty 3D space waiting to be filled.
* **Text:** *"You are the Architect. Build your web. Push back the dark."*
* **Button:** `[ Ignited First Cluster ]`

## Part 2: The Daily "Mini-Stories"

Don't use standard error messages or empty states. Use the story to guide behavior.

#### A. When the App is Empty (Zero State)

* **Visual:** A lonely, drifting particle effect.
* **Text:** *"The canvas is silent. It waits for a Spark."*
* **Button:** `[ + Upload Knowledge ]` (Instead of "Create Deck")

#### B. The "Fade" (Why you need to review)

Instead of a notification saying *"You have 20 cards due,"* use the narrative:

* **Morning Notification:** *"The Physics Cluster is dimming. The Fade is creeping in."*
* **In-App Visual:** The "Physics" node is gray and pulsing slowly. The links are turning into dotted lines (breaking).
* **The Mission:** *"Repair the pathways before they break."*

#### C. The Victory (After a Review Session)

* **Visual:** A wave of energy ripples out from the node you just finished, illuminating the nearby nodes for a second.
* **Text:** *"The web is stabilized. Brilliance restored."*
* **Reward:** `+50 Lumen` (The currency of light).

---

## Part 3: The "Lumen" Economy (Gamification Story)

To explain the "Free Limit" (3 AI generations/day) without sounding greedy, we use the physics of this world.

**The Concept:**
Creating something from nothing (AI Generation) requires massive energy. Maintaining what you have (Reviewing) generates energy.

**The Narrative Rule:**
*"To birth a new star, you must harvest the light of the old ones."*

**The Loop:**

1. **Review your cards** (Recharge the web) $\rightarrow$ You earn **Lumen**.
2. **Use Lumen** to fire the **"Genesis Engine"** (The AI).
3. **The Limit:** Free users have a small "Battery." They can only store enough Lumen for 3 Genesis events a day. Pro users have an "Infinite Core."

---

## Part 4: The "Focus Flow" (Pomodoro Story)

When the user enters Deep Focus mode:

* **The Story:** *"The outside world is noise. We are entering the Core."*
* **Visual:** The background blurs. The UI disappears. Only the current Spark and its Link are visible.
* **The Timer:** It’s not a clock. It’s a **Ring of Light** orbiting the Spark. As time passes, the ring closes.
* **Completion:** When the ring closes, the Spark "Evolves" (changes shape or gets a permanent halo).
* **The Message:** *"Orbit complete. Evolution locked in."*
